Papa's Flight Log

Two logbooks, ten years, two wars. Every line below was transcribed from his handwritten entries. I, and Claude, did my best to transcribe them but they may not be perfect. All the flight paths are obviously fabricated, but the general vicinities should all be correct. Scroll to follow him from his first solo in 1943 to his last flight in 1953. Click or tap any entry to see where the flight, the plane Papa flew in, and the logbook page it was written on. The maps are indicative of geopolitical boundaries at the time.

Places marked as estimated were matched from his spellings and checked against flight times; the evidence for each is in the project's data/places.csv. Flight tracks are illustrations: the logbooks record where he flew, not the path. Battles shown with approximate positions are marked so.
